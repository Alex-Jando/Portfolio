Built with Chris Li, Ben Rosa, and Sophia Bianchi for the Google Developer
Group Mac-a-thon at McMaster, Carbon Pals reframed a broad climate problem as
something a person could act on today. An onboarding questionnaire estimated a
baseline footprint, then Gemini generated ten tailored daily tasks.

The product loop connected those recommendations to streaks, progress charts,
friends, communities, and an activity feed. Optional photo proof was resized
and compressed before being stored, keeping a social feature practical within
a fast-moving hackathon build.
